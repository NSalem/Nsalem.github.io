About

I am a cognitive (neuro)scientist studying motivation, decisions, and beliefs.

My research addresses questions such as:

  • How do we combine information (sensory evidence, experience, and subjective values) to make decisions?
  • How do we form and update our beliefs about the world and ourselves?
  • How do we monitor uncertainty (in the environment and our own brains) to guide our behavior?
  • How do we balance flexibility and stability in decision-making and learning?

I address these questions using behavioral experiments, computational modeling, and EEG.
